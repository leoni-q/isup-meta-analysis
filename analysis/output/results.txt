==============================================================================
PART 2. POOLED PROPORTIONS BY SCREENING STRATEGY (GLMM)
==============================================================================
Each trial contributes at most one independent unit to a strategy.
Same-strategy arms within a trial are combined before model fitting.
PROBASE contributes only its immediate-PSA arm.
Detection rates are reported on both denominators required to read
them: per man screened (protocol s.9) and per man randomized/invited.
Grade mix uses all detected prostate cancers as denominator.
Biopsy yield uses outcome-specific biopsy denominators from the CSV.
Strategies are fitted separately; no all-strategy estimate or test is run.

CAVEAT on per-screened detection rates: in ERSPC, PLCO and CAP the
numerators are cancers accrued across the whole randomized arm over
long follow-up, whereas the per-screened denominator counts only men
actually tested. Per-screened rates are therefore upward-biased for
those trials, and the per-randomized/invited rates are the internally
consistent ones. Both are reported so the difference is visible.

Study-strategy units:
  No screening / usual care    ERSPC (C), PLCO (C), CAP (C)
  PSA + DRE                    PLCO (I)
  PSA                          ERSPC (I), STHLM3-MRI (C), MVP (C), PROBASE (I), CAP (I)
  PSA -> MRI                   GOTEBORG-2 (I+C), PROSA (C)
  Biomarker -> MRI             STHLM3-MRI (I)
  MRI-first                    MVP (I), PROSA (I)

==============================================================================
PROTOCOL DEVIATIONS AND UNRESOLVED ITEMS
==============================================================================
Deviations from protocol.md are recorded here so they can be reported
alongside the results rather than discovered on comparison.

1. Stratification of comparative analyses (protocol s.8).
   The protocol specifies pooling risk ratios 'stratified by the
   screening strategy'. A risk ratio is a contrast BETWEEN two
   strategies, so a single strategy label cannot describe it. Trials
   are therefore grouped by contrast family: screening invitation vs
   no screening (A), diagnostic/biopsy strategy with both arms
   screened (B), and timing of screening initiation (T). Families are
   never pooled with one another. Single-arm proportions ARE
   stratified by screening strategy exactly as the protocol states.

2. Continuity correction for proportions (protocol s.8).
   The protocol states a 0.5 correction for zero cells in both
   analyses. It is applied to the risk-ratio models. The proportion
   models are logit-normal GLMMs fitted by likelihood, which use the
   exact binomial likelihood and require no continuity correction;
   adding one would bias them. No correction is therefore applied on
   the proportion side.

3. Curative-treatment outcome (protocol s.2 and s.9).
   'Percentage of patients receiving curative treatment over the
   course of follow-up' is a registered secondary outcome. The
   included reports do not provide extractable arm-level treatment
   counts, so no data column exists and the outcome is NOT analysed.
   This is an outcome-level deviation and must be reported as such.

4. Adverse events and procedural harms (protocol s.9).
   Contextual data only, not formal endpoints; not extracted into the
   analysis dataset and therefore not summarized here.

5. Exclusion of an extracted trial.
   ProScreen was extracted (arm_level_data.csv) but is excluded from
   every analysis, including the descriptive tables.
