Grade-mix outcomes use all detected cancers as denominator.
Biopsy-yield outcomes use outcome-specific biopsy denominators from
the supplied CSV and require data for both randomized arms.
Invitation and diagnostic-strategy contrasts are fitted separately;
no pooled estimate is calculated across incompatible contrast families.
PROBASE is reported separately as a timing-of-screening contrast.
CAP remains in invitation-family models; its precision is not adjusted
for practice-level clustering. Excluding-CAP models are in Part 3.
