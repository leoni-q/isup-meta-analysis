PART 2. POOLED PROPORTIONS BY SCREENING STRATEGY (GLMM)
==============================================================================
Arms are classified by the triage test that triggers work-up. Denominators
are the number of men actually screened where reported; control arms of
invitation trials were not screened and retain the invited/randomized N.

IMPORTANT CAVEAT: in ERSPC, PLCO and CAP the numerators are cancers
accrued across the whole randomized arm over 9-10 years of follow-up,
whereas the per-screened denominator counts only men actually tested.
These per-screened rates are therefore upward-biased for those three
trials. Part 3 repeats the analysis on ITT denominators.

Arms per strategy group:
  No screening / usual care    ERSPC-C, PLCO-C, CAP-C, ProScreen-C

  Biomarker -> MRI             ProScreen-I, STHLM3-MRI-I
  MRI-first                    MVP-I, PROSA-I
